# FAQ

## Is Muffin available to install publicly?

Not yet. Muffin is a private developer preview under DAY-1 hardening. The public
documentation is being aligned before a trusted alpha and eventual public alpha.

## Is Muffin open source?

The project intends to become open source, but the active runtime is not yet a
public release. The release Gate includes install, update, recovery, security and
contributor experience, not only source visibility.

## Is Muffin fully local?

The runtime and canonical continuity are owner-run. Model inference may use an
explicitly configured cloud provider. Muffin's claim is owner control,
inspectable egress and provider portability—not that every computation is local
today.

## Is Muffin a productivity assistant?

Productivity is not its identity, but real work is part of the product. Mail,
calendar, browser, files and processes are coherent future capabilities when
they reduce direct interface work without removing owner judgement and control.

## What makes Muffin different from a chat with memory?

Muffin treats continuity as more than retrieved facts. Evidence, beliefs,
unfinished work, possible effects and authority remain distinct and survive
sessions and process boundaries.

## Which interfaces exist today?

CLI, one-shot headless execution and Telegram are current. Desktop, mobile,
voice, wearable and remote Node clients are planned directions.

## Does better understanding give Muffin more permission?

No. Understanding and authority are separate. The model may infer or propose;
policy and owner decisions govern what can execute.

## What happened to the awareness loop and dream cycle?

They belong to an earlier Muffin generation. The product properties behind some
of those ideas—continuity, calibrated presence, learning from correction—remain
interesting, but the old mechanisms are not current runtime claims.

## Why preserve the old documents?

They show the project's research lineage and changes of mind. Removing them
would make the history look cleaner but less truthful. They remain available as
explicitly labelled historical material.

## Where is the exact engineering status?

The active runtime repository owns the volatile Gate and implementation state.
This public repository intentionally exposes stable maturity labels rather than
private operational detail or counts that can become stale within a day.
