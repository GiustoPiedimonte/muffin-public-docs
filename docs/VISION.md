# Vision

> **Status: VISION** — this document is allowed to be ahead of the runtime.

Muffin aims to become one continuous personal agent that its owner runs under
their control. A chat, model, process, device or interface is a replaceable body
around that continuity, not the identity of the agent.

## The product bet

Memory, tools, self-hosting and messaging are becoming baseline agent features.
Muffin's durable bet is narrower:

> **quality, ownership and portability of personal continuity.**

Continuity includes what happened, who said what, what changed, what remains
unfinished, what may already have happened in the world and which authority the
agent currently holds.

It should survive a model change, a provider change, a process restart, a new
surface and eventually a move to another owner-controlled machine.

## Do · understand · be present

Muffin should:

- **do** real work, including multi-step and long-running work;
- **understand** by keeping evidence, beliefs, provenance, contradiction and
  uncertainty distinct;
- **be present** by preserving obligations across sessions, waiting when needed,
  resuming safely and choosing silence when there is no earned reason to speak.

An executor with no continuity is a tool. A memory that cannot act is a
notebook. An assistant whose obligations disappear with the session is not
continuous.

## Human-first

Muffin should make the owner more capable without making the owner less agentic.
It can carry memory, comparison, tracking and repeatable execution while the
owner retains judgement, correction, interruption and control.

Cognitive-science ideas may identify useful problems, but they are not software
blueprints or product guarantees. Mechanisms such as the older dream cycle or
Predictor/Scheduler/Decider loop remain historical experiments unless current
evidence earns a new implementation.

## Owner-run, not provider-bound

Muffin's continuity should remain under owner control. That does not require all
inference to be local: an owner may deliberately choose a cloud model. The
important boundary is that provider egress is explicit and replaceable, while
the canonical continuity remains portable.

## Future bodies and capabilities

CLI and Telegram are current surfaces. Desktop, voice, mobile, wearables and
remote execution Nodes are possible future bodies, not current claims.

Capabilities should grow around a narrow trusted core. Installing a package in
the future should not silently grant every power it can request; authority must
remain explicit, reviewable and local to the owner's installation.

## Success

Success is not the number of integrations or the amount of automation. It is how
much of the owner's digital life Muffin can carry without losing continuity,
understanding, control or the ability to intervene.
